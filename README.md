1. **Visit the Website**: Go to [https://spendgatesmoney.com](https://spendgatesmoney.com)

4. **Track Your Progress**: Monitor your balance and spending statistics

- **Buying Items**: Click the "Buy" button or enter a quantity in the input field

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is this game free to play?**  
A: Yes! Spend Bill Gates Money is completely free to play. No registration, no downloads, no payments required.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**  
A: No account needed! The game runs entirely in your browser. Your progress is saved locally on your device.

**Q: Can I play on mobile devices?**  
A: Absolutely! The game is fully responsive and works great on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.

**Q: How long does it take to spend all the money?**  
A: It depends on what you buy! With $130+ billion, you could spend millions per day for years. Try it and see how long it takes you!

### Gameplay Questions

**Q: Can I sell items back?**  
A: Yes! You can sell any items you've purchased to recover funds. Just click the "Sell" button on any item you own.

**Q: What happens when I run out of money?**  
A: When you spend everything, you'll see a special celebration modal with your statistics. You can then reset and start over!

**Q: Can I buy multiple items at once?**  
A: Yes! You can enter a quantity in the input field or click Buy/Sell multiple times. The game will automatically calculate the total cost.

**Q: Is there a way to save my progress?**  
A: Your progress is automatically saved in your browser's local storage. As long as you use the same browser and device, your purchases will be remembered.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why isn't the game loading?**  
A: Make sure you're using a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge). Try clearing your browser cache or disabling ad blockers.

**Q: Can I play offline?**  
A: The game requires an internet connection to load initially, but once loaded, you can play without internet for basic gameplay.

**Q: Are my purchases shared with other players?**  
A: No, all gameplay is local to your device. Your purchases and progress are private and not shared.

### About the Money

**Q: Are these real net worth figures?**  
A: The amounts are based on estimated net worth figures, but they may vary as real-world fortunes fluctuate. The game is for entertainment purposes.

**Q: Why are the amounts so large?**  
A: Billionaire fortunes are genuinely this large! The game helps put these astronomical numbers into perspective.

**Q: How accurate are the item prices?**  
A: Prices are approximate and based on real-world estimates. Some items may have simplified pricing for gameplay purposes.
